Hit To Destroy: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

In short: Hit To Destroy does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases, and it works fully offline.

Hit To Destroy is a game for Android developed by Hobby App Box. This policy explains what information the game handles.

Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal or device information. The game does not ask you to sign in, does not use the internet, and does not include analytics, crash reporting or advertising software.

Data Stored on Your Device

Your game progress (levels won, stars, coins, crew levels and your saved forts) and your settings (sound, music, vibration and graphics) are saved only on your device. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else.

  • To erase your progress, open Settings in the game and tap Reset progress.
  • Uninstalling the game removes all of its saved data from your device.

Permissions

The game only asks to use your phone's vibration, for short buzzes when your castle is hit. You can turn vibration off in Settings.

Advertising and Purchases

The game has no ads and no in-app purchases.

Children's Privacy

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children under 13 either.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
