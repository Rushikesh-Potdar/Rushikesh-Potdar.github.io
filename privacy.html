Privacy Policy for Star Dash

Last updated: 26 August 2026

Star Dash is a single-player Android game. This policy explains what data the game handles and what the third-party advertising service it uses collects.

Data the game collects

None. Star Dash has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of its own. It does not collect, transmit, or store your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, or any other personal information.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress locally on your device so it persists between sessions. This includes:

  • High score and level progress
  • Credits earned and ships unlocked
  • Your selected ship
  • Sound and music preferences

This data never leaves your device and is not visible to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising

Star Dash displays ads through Google AdMob, which shows banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads. To serve ads, Google may collect and process certain information, including your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type and operating system, and interactions with the ads shown.

This collection is performed by Google, not by us. Its handling is governed by Google's own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from AdMob partners

Your advertising choices

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation entirely, in your Android settings under Settings → Google → Ads. Doing so does not remove ads, but makes them non-personalised.

If you are in a region covered by the GDPR or similar rules, the game shows a consent prompt from Google's User Messaging Platform the first time it runs, letting you choose how your data is used for advertising. You can be shown this choice again by clearing the game's data or reinstalling it.

Children

Star Dash is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children. The ad content rating requested from AdMob is set to be appropriate for the game's Play Store audience rating.

Changes to this policy
