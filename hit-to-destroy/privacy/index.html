Hit To Destroy: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

In short: Hit To Destroy has no accounts and no in-app purchases, and we do not collect any personal information ourselves. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and AdMob may collect some device information to show and measure those ads, as described below.

Hit To Destroy is a game for Android developed by Hobby App Box. This policy explains what information the game handles.

Data We Collect Directly

We do not collect any personal information. The game does not ask you to sign in and does not include analytics or crash-reporting software of its own.

Data Stored on Your Device

Your game progress (levels won, stars, coins, crew levels and your saved forts) and your settings (sound, music, vibration and graphics) are saved only on your device. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else.

  • To erase your progress, open Settings in the game and tap Reset progress.
  • Uninstalling the game removes all of its saved data from your device.

Permissions

The game uses your phone's vibration for short buzzes when your castle is hit (you can turn it off in Settings), and internet access only to load ads. The game itself plays fully offline.

Advertising

The game shows ads using Google AdMob: a full-screen ad now and then between battles, and an optional ad you can choose to watch to double your battle rewards. Ads are never shown during a battle.

To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, Google AdMob may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (and the approximate location it implies), and how you interact with the ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

For how Google uses this information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases

The game has no in-app purchases.

Children's Privacy

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
